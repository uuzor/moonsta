# Pitch deck outline (8 slides, build in Slides/Canva)

1. **Title** — Moonsta: your money copilot that never sleeps. One line: chat-native
   AI that protects your Solana capital 24/7. Bot handle + QR to t.me link.
2. **Problem** — people get liquidated and rugged while asleep. Setting up
   protection today = dashboards + untrusted bots + screen-watching. (One stat
   or anecdote if you have one; else the 3am story.)
3. **Solution** — your Solana money copilot in Telegram: paste an address
   and get three jobs in one chat. **Protect** (one-tap stop-losses, enforced
   24/7), **Find** (reports, risk scores, quotes, briefings), **Act**
   (approve-to-execute inline). Screenshots: /start, report, guard
   suggestion, approval card, dashboard.
4. **How it works** — diagram: chat → agent (Muse Spark) → typed tools →
   Solana (reads/Jupiter/Drift) → policy engine → execute-or-propose →
   notify. Note: deterministic guards, LLM does language only.
5. **Safety (judges probe here)** — pre-authorized bounded actions, caps +
   allowlists, tx simulation + structural verification, withdrawals ALWAYS
   approved, /pause kill switch, audit log. Custody path: hot wallet →
   sessions → MPC.
6. **Solana depth** — live reads, Jupiter V2 execution, Drift monitoring,
   on-chain risk signals. Not a wallet-connect button: every number on screen
   comes from chain.
7. **Demo** — 2–4 min video embedded/linked. Crash-protection moment first.
8. **Traction/next** — what's live today; next: research digest, Drift
   execution, verified wallets, NEAR cross-chain leg. Team + contact.

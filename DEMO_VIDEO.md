# Demo video script (target 3:00, hard cap 4:00)

Record on a phone: Telegram open, screen-record. Narrate clearly. Do ONE
rehearsal first (see crash prep below).

- **0:00–0:20 — Hook.** "It's 3am. SOL dumps 12% while you sleep. By morning
  you're down — unless something was awake for you. This is Moonsta."
- **0:20–0:50 — Onboarding.** /start the bot, paste a wallet address, show the
  connected reply. "No signup, no extension, thirty seconds."
- **0:50–1:20 — Report + risk.** Ask for a full report; show holdings. Ask to
  score a token; show the risk score + suggested stop-loss. "Every number is
  read live from Solana."
- **1:20–2:00 — THE crash moment.** Show the armed guard ("protect SOL").
  Trigger the pre-seeded drop alert (it fires within ~a minute on the live
  worker). Show the notification arriving: what fired, what the agent did,
  amounts. "I was asleep. It wasn't."
- **2:00–2:30 — Approvals.** Show a swap proposal card, tap Approve, show the
  executed signature. Mention: withdrawals always need approval, /pause
  freezes everything.
- **2:30–3:00 — Copilot + close.** Ask one "find" question on camera ("score
  this token" or "quote 1 SOL to USDC"). Open the Mini App: total + chart,
  guards with toggles, approvals. Close: "Protection, research, and trading
  — one copilot in your pocket. Try it now at the link below."

## Crash prep (do 10 min before recording)

1. Connect the demo wallet in the bot.
2. Tell the builder (me) your Telegram numeric id — I'll seed a price-drop
   alert rule that is already breaching, so the live worker fires it on its
   next 15s tick and the notification lands ~1 min later via the drain.
3. Rehearse once end-to-end, then record. Keep the worker in dry-run: the
   protection is demonstrated through real quotes + simulated execution
   (safe on mainnet reads, no funds move without your tap).
